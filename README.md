🛡️ UAC-CareAI
Intelligent System Capacity & Care-Load Analytics Platform

UAC-CareAI is an analytics and machine-learning platform designed to understand system capacity, care load, operational pressure, unusual activity, recurring patterns, and future load conditions from UAC operational data.

🎯 What does this project do?

The platform converts operational data into easy-to-understand insights that help answer questions such as:

📊 How much load is currently present in the system?

📈 Is the operational pressure increasing or decreasing?

🔄 How are transfers and discharges affecting the system?

🚨 Are there unusual operational conditions?

🧩 What operational patterns are occurring repeatedly?

🔮 What could the future system load look like?

🧠 Which factors are influencing the machine-learning predictions?

The platform doesn't only provide predictions.
